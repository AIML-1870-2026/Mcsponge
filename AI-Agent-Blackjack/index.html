♠ AI Agent Blackjack

Getting Started

  • Upload a .env file containing OPENAI_API_KEY=sk-...
  • Click Refresh Models to load available Claude models
  • Select a model family and model, set your bet, then click PLAY

Using the AI Agent

  • After each deal or hit, the AI automatically recommends an action
  • Click Execute Recommendation to follow it, or choose your own
  • The AI returns structured JSON: {"action":"hit","reasoning":"..."}

Rules

  • Starting balance: $1,000
  • Blackjack pays 1.5× your bet
  • Dealer stands on 17+, hits on 16 or less
  • Double — available on your first two cards only
  • Split — available when you hold a pair (plays one new hand)
  • Insurance — available when dealer shows an Ace; costs half your bet, pays 2:1 if dealer has blackjack

Privacy

  • API key is held in memory only — never stored or logged
